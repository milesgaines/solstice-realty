## What "hitmaker" refers to

**HitMakerDistro** (hitmakerdistro.com) — the music distributor used for Miles
Gaines releases. Mail arrives from three vendor senders:

| Sender | What it sends |
|--------|---------------|
| `release@hitmakerdistro.com` | "Yes! We have received your release!" submission receipts, and "Release approved" notices |
| `no-reply@hitmakerdistro.com` | Monthly sales reports / payout credit notices |
| `info@hitmakerdistro.com` | Older monthly sales reports (used through ~7/2025) |
| `support@hitmakerdistro.com` | Support inbox (outbound requests) |

Submission receipts and reports go to the business admin address, cc'd to the
personal Gmail.

## Timeline observed

- **Monthly sales reports** run continuously from 7/2025 through **5/2026**
